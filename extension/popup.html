Auto-scan pages

Find QR codes without clicking. Pages are scanned when they load, after you scroll, and while a video plays. Chrome asks for access to all sites when you turn this on.

Auto-scan stays off because Chrome didn't get access to sites.

While a video plays, scan every

Every second finds codes in videos sooner but uses about twice as much CPU.

Sites where auto-scan is off

None. To turn auto-scan off for a site, right-click the QR Reader icon while on it.

    To scan without opening this, press Alt+Shift+Q.
